# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## Durable Product Decisions

- Each history script owns exactly one module at `src/data/historySeeds/scripts/<seed-id>/index.ts`. Shared construction and tags stay in `src/data/historySeeds/shared.ts`, while `src/data/historySeeds/index.ts` is the only explicit catalogue aggregation point. Additions or removals must update both the script directory and the index, keep order intentional, and pass the module-structure test; never collapse the catalogue back into one monolithic seed file.
- Maintain one authoritative full-product code line on `main`; do not keep a separate review-lite catalogue or product branch. The normal product always uses all 100 scripts. Every Douyin Interactive Space package must be produced by `npm run build:interactive`, which swaps only the build-time history catalogue to Gutenberg printing, Galileo's *Sidereus Nuncius*, and Apollo 11, removes every other history image, rejects any leaked non-review script ID, enforces the 8MB ZIP ceiling, and emits the review ZIP. Never hand-package or submit a general 100-script build to Douyin.
- The product title is exactly `哎！我改变了历史？`.
- The history picker header uses the project-owned transparent calligraphic wordmark at `/assets/brand/history-wordmark.png`; keep the exact title as its accessible name and do not restore the instructional subtitle `选择你要闯入的瞬间`. Keep only one aligned 44px settings button in the header; random draw, unlocked archive, rules announcement, and audio belong in its accessible secondary menu rather than appearing as separate top-level controls.
- The first screen is a destiny draw over all 100 historical moments, not a free-choice catalogue. Before a draw, show one unrevealed physical card. After the player acts, animate an evenly paced 3D carousel with no late deceleration, made from the existing full-size archival-poster `HistoryCard` components themselves: the next card turns in from the right-rear plane, becomes the one sharp front card, and the previous card rotates away toward the left-rear plane. Keep side/background cards low-contrast so only one card competes for attention; never remount three equally bright flat posters, fade/blur the whole stage on every tick, or fake the draw by moving only a separate top timeline. The carousel must settle on exactly one node while the year readout follows it. The active poster's width, scene height, dossier height, and scale must remain identical across the last spin and the settled state, with no extra settle-scale animation or size snap. The settled card keeps its illustrated scene, vermilion year rail, warm-paper dossier, complete place/role/decision/deadline, and separate `闯入这一刻` action.
- Destiny-draw geometry must be relative to the 320-430px product container, never to the outer browser viewport. The active timeline dot, year readout, and revealed poster must always name the same seed. Random selection prioritizes histories that have not been unlocked and avoids the immediately previous result when possible.
- The player is a modern Chinese traveler, not an abstract observer. Do not make the player choose among 100 starts before play; there is no profile, personality test, MBTI label, or recommended subset.
- Opening cards must be famous, concrete historical turning points, including clearly labelled BCE dates where appropriate, with an exact date, place, assigned role, immediate decision, deadline, and actual outcome.
- The deck must include substantial Chinese history before 1840 and frame world history in language accessible to Chinese players.
- The one-hundred-card deck is fixed at exactly 58 Chinese moments and 42 world moments. Every Chinese moment must occur before 1949; do not include events from 1949 onward in China or political subjects centered on the Chinese Communist Party, the People's Republic of China, or "New China." Soviet history remains allowed as world history. World cards must use people or events broadly recognizable to ordinary Chinese players; do not spend slots on minor-country or specialist Western history.
- The Interactive Space release is the complete 100-history roguelike product, not a three-script review subset. `npm run build:interactive` must package all 100 openings, four decisions, three Rolls per node, full endings, local art, and music. The real upload endpoint rejects ZIPs larger than 8MB even when the provided validator passes, so keep mobile asset optimization and the pre-ZIP size guard; never hand-package an ordinary web build. Keep every runtime asset local and relative, route AI only through `tt.callAIChatCompletion`, configure publishing through `interative_content_mcp`, and never embed an API key in source or the upload package. `interactive-space.release.json` is the publishing contract: AI stays enabled, the Volcengine Model ID stays exactly `deepseek-v4-flash-260425`, credentials stay in the platform's Volcengine API-key configuration, and any `20107` credential failure blocks submission. The homepage title corner keeps the quiet accessible `AI 生成` disclosure, but event scenes and both final reports do not repeat model/source badges such as `AI 辅助创作 · 固定开场` or `AI 生成 · V4 Flash`; the Interactive build calls the announcement `体验说明`, and every release ZIP must pass both the repository verifier and the user-provided official `h5-validator` before upload.
- Interactive Space copy must describe the complete package that is actually running: history counts derive from the full build-time catalogue and must say 100 playable openings.
- Public-memory fiction may inspire a counterfactual hook, but the card itself must label and anchor real history. Preserve dramatic choices without turning sexual violence into a playable entertainment mechanic.
- All 100 history moments belong to one random draw pool. The instant the fourth decision becomes client-owned canon, permanently unlock that opening independently of whether the two AI ending reports succeed; keep unlocked IDs in their own durable local collection so session corruption, retry, restart, or storage-version migration cannot erase them. The settings menu exposes only completed moments in a secondary `已解锁档案`, never the full locked catalogue as a free-choice browser. Every unlocked archive is replayable: selecting it clears the prior run and starts that exact history again from its fixed first turn.
- The run has one authoritative timeline containing only the player's decisions. Never generate a hidden personality timeline or let a personality system choose on the player's behalf.
- Active play is card-only. Never expose a free-text decision, direct-rewrite sheet, custom result path, or fourth decision entry. A legacy saved timeline may still retain an older player-authored fact for continuity, but no current UI may create a new one.
- Every node initially owns six complete choice objects generated in one model response: the opening trio plus a separately authored `rollChoices` trio for the first prepared Roll. Each trio is ordered as A `循史` (execute the scene's already-moving trajectory and make its result happen on time), B `破局` (change that trajectory's controller, command direction, or result), and C `天外` (a genuinely reality-changing counterfactual). `循史` never means mild, passive, or morally safer: if the historical/current trajectory is a war, coup, execution, refusal, or institutional revolution, A must still carry it out concretely. A may not block, reverse, delay, replace the controlling actor, or quietly change the result; B must change at least one of those facts.
- Choosing A `循史` adds zero new historical deviation: on the fixed opening it leaves deviation at 0, and on later alternate-world scenes it preserves the already accumulated deviation without increasing it. B and C are the only card classes that add new deviation.
- `天外` is driven by the authoritative 50-item catalogue in `src/game/powers.ts`. The client draws powers without replacement for the whole run and persists the remaining deck, used IDs, next-turn pair, and failed live-Roll assignment so refresh or retry never changes the promised power. Every C choice must copy the exact client-assigned `powerId`, use only that ability, and set `actionSpec.actor` exactly to `你`; historical people may be targets, allies, opponents, or affected parties, but never replace the player as the power user. A/B must not carry `powerId`. The model must combine the power with named people, objects, orders, places, and deadlines from the current snapshot; costs may complicate success but never cancel it.
- Fixed first turns stay zero-wait: each of the 100 snapshots owns two development-time AI-authored A/B trios in `src/data/fixedOpeningChoices.generated.ts` plus six AI-authored C candidates in `src/data/fixedPowerChoices.generated.ts`. The A cards are independently checked against the seed's actual historical outcome; the B cards must change it. `npm run generate:fixed-openings` and `npm run generate:fixed-powers` regenerate the same authoritative full sources used by both web and Interactive Space builds. Runtime randomly chooses two distinct C candidates for the opening and prepared Roll without adding a network wait. Do not restore generic deterministic A/B templates, a small surreal template list, or abstractions such as `让谎言现形`, `历史现场所有谎言`, `唤醒器物作证`, or co-actors like `被迫说真话的在场者`.
- The player sees exactly three cards at once and may Roll three times per node. The first Roll reveals the prepared `rollChoices` only after a roughly 1.2-second physical shuffle ritual, even though it needs no network request. The second and third Rolls each request a new A/B/C trio from the model against every card already seen in that scene; they must enter the same visible shuffle/waiting state before either new cards or an error appears, hide any stale error immediately on retry, keep the current scene fixed, remain retryable on failure, and persist `rollCount`, the latest dynamic trio, and any in-flight request across refresh.
- A choice has two intentionally different texts: `displayLabel` is a 4-12 character card face (schema ceiling 16), while `label` plus `actionSpec` and `instantEcho` are the complete canonical decision used by continuity, the next model call, and the long-press detail sheet. Never replace the canonical label with the card face.
- Preserve the current coal, newsprint, vermilion, teal, and yellow visual system unless the user explicitly requests a new visual direction.
- A complete run has exactly four playable decisions within one protagonist's biological lifetime. The alternate 2026 report is a separate posthumous epilogue and never consumes one of the four nodes.
- The protagonist keeps one fixed name and body across all four nodes, ages monotonically, may change role, faction, city, and social reach, and dies after the fourth decision. Never use consciousness relay, reincarnation, descendants, or a replacement identity as the playable character.
- The first two nodes provide tight feedback on the same day and three days later. The third is a consequential mid-life turning point, and the fourth is the final late-life decision; modern starts compress the lifespan so death still occurs before the 2026 report.
- After node 3, the opening event cannot remain the current plot, title, objective, or role. It may be named once as a causal source when the current title, task, role, institution, and conflict have genuinely changed. The same protagonist must enter a different consequential conflict caused by earlier choices rather than randomly changing topic.
- Surprise comes from changing the social carrier, conflict, institution, or affected population, not from forced travel. A timeline may remain in China; prefer real people, institutions, cities, and lived experiences familiar to Chinese players, while changing at least two narrative dimensions from the recent three nodes.
- Active gameplay always provides an exit control that clears the current run and returns to the destiny draw while preserving permanent unlocked history IDs.
- Event screens target the mainstream portrait browser range from 360 × 667 through 430 × 932, not only the iPhone 13 viewport. The primary page shows the scene, complete event copy, the full three-card table, the compact three-use Roll control, and one secondary historical-context trigger. Prompt-target turns must fit with every primary control visible without scrolling across that range. The client imposes no character ceiling on complete AI-authored historical prose and preserves truly oversized overflow for scrolling. Do not restore free input, a fourth path, the four-metric dashboard, or repeated mission explanations.
- Scene art must follow the simulated year as well as the chapter. Never show modern or space technology in a pre-industrial node merely because it is a late chapter.
- A refresh or development-server reconnect during generation must resume the saved AI request automatically. Harmless non-narrative drift may be normalized locally, but visible historical prose is always AI-authored. A parseable response repairs only its invalid top-level AI fields and merges them back into the untouched scene; field-level validation must report missing fields and invalid narrative fields together so one compact patch can fix both. If that patch still fails, allow one evidence-guided full recovery; after three invalid AI outputs, preserve the run and show an explicit retry instead of inventing a generic local historical scene.
- DeepSeek uses phase-sized output ceilings: 4096 tokens for a complete turn and its six cards, 2048 for each of the two concurrent ending reports. The stable system protocol and changing user payload must stay compact: every input fact needs a live continuity, validation, or visible-story consumer, and the client never sends fields it can authoritatively derive. The low-latency wire removes only client-known repetition: each generated turn returns one nine-position scene array plus two three-card arrays; the client injects actor `你`, copies the full canonical label into `actionSpec.action`, inherits the scene deadline, derives the two historical anchors from the AI-authored location and role, and preserves every visible AI-authored decision/result string.
- Loading-room motion includes archival drift, causal-thread tracing, pulse nodes, exposure passes, and status cycling, with a `prefers-reduced-motion` static fallback.
- Loading progress is monotonic and honest: connected, reasoning, writing, validating, and repairing states come from the actual streamed request rather than timers. The steps and track never loop back or pretend to reach 100%. Use `新历史正在成形` rather than `因果推演` as the player-facing frame.
- Each of the 100 history cards owns a fixed, schema-validated first turn with both three-card trios and enters it immediately without a model request. Turns 2-4 and both final reports remain model-generated, but active scenes and reports do not expose generation-source or model-version labels. Local deterministic code must never invent a continuation scene or a 2026 report.
- Every history seed owns a local `/assets/history/<seed-id>.webp` path with an event-accurate source or a local period-art fallback; broken remote images must never be part of runtime rendering.
- The home year readout is a theatrical randomization indicator, not a manually selectable navigation control. While drawing, the full-height archival cards themselves rotate through a perspective carousel at one constant cadence with linear per-card motion and no final slowdown; after stopping, the year readout and centered poster expose one matching result without changing the poster's geometry.
- The revealed history card is one continuous poster: the scene and ragged dossier share the same width, the dossier overlaps the scene, and the action remains a related but separate vermilion slab. Keep this existing card style unchanged when evolving the draw animation.
- The destiny home must fit the masked or revealed card, primary draw/redraw action, and draw guidance inside one unscrolled viewport from 360 × 667 through 430 × 932. During rotation, reserve the whole stage for the cards and one quiet status line: do not show `闯入这一刻`, `换一个开局`, or a disabled draw button until the carousel has stopped.
